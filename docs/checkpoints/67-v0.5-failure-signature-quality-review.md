## Result

Breakpoint 3 passes after one contract correction. Rust `E0308` normalization
previously scraped expected/found types from free-form diagnostic prose. The
evidence contract now carries paired `expected_type` and `actual_type` fields,
and the normalizer refuses missing, partial, or unsafe values. Raw diagnostic
messages no longer contribute meaning to any signature.

requires both a typed verification target and a workspace-relative diagnostic
path. It remains visibly path/target-specific and reports the absent symbol;
it does not fall back to target-only equivalence.
